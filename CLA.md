## How to sign

When You open your first pull request, a bot asks You to sign. Read this agreement, then reply on the pull request with this exact comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records Your GitHub username, the date and the pull request in `signatures/cla.json`, on the `cla-signatures` branch of this repository. You sign once; it covers all Your later Contributions. If the check does not update, comment `recheck`.
